        Tips for a good arrow

        • Hold the phone flat in front of you.
        • If the arrow spins or looks wrong, wave the phone in a figure 8 a few times.
        • Metal lockers and speakers can throw the compass off. Step away from them for a moment.
        • Save spots near windows or doors, where GPS works better. Walking outside for a minute lets the app tune the compass and learn your stride.
        • On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app.
        • Lost the arrow? Look for a Class Compass QR poster and scan it with your camera.
        • The home-screen app keeps its own list. If you saved classes in Safari first, copy the share link there and paste it under "Add classes from a link" in the home-screen app.

        Class Compass counts app use anonymously. No names or locations are sent.
